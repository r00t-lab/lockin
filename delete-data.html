Deleting your data

Last updated 20 August 2026

Nagg has no accounts, so there is no profile to close and no login to revoke. Almost everything Nagg knows about you is on your phone and nowhere else. This page says exactly what exists, where, and how to get rid of it.

The short version. Uninstall the app and everything Nagg stored is gone with it. The only record that lives anywhere else is the one behind a subscription, and we delete that on request by email.

Everything on the device: uninstall

On iPhone, press and hold the Nagg icon ▸ Remove App ▸ Delete App. On Android, press and hold the icon ▸ Uninstall, or Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Nagg ▸ Uninstall.

That removes your commitments, their times and proof settings, your streaks, your missed count, your desk codes and the scheduled alarm chain. None of it was ever copied off the phone, so nothing is left behind once the app is gone.

Proof photos need no deletion. A photo of your desk is examined on the device to check the frame is not empty and is then discarded — it is never uploaded, never written to your photo library, and never kept by Nagg.

What is not on your phone

  • RevenueCat, which runs subscriptions for us, holds an anonymous identifier generated on your device and the purchase receipt the store issued. It has never been given your name, your email, your commitments or your photos. If you have never bought anything, there is nothing there to delete.
  • Apple and Google keep their own record of any purchase you made. That record is the store's, not ours; we cannot reach into it or delete it, and their policies govern it.

Asking us to delete the subscription record

Email info@kivanckarahasan.pro with the subject Delete my data, and tell us two things: whether you bought through the App Store or Google Play, and the order or transaction number from your purchase receipt.

We ask for that number because there is no account here — without it we have no way to tell which anonymous identifier is yours, and we would rather admit that than pretend to search. Apple's receipts are at reportaproblem.apple.com; Google's are under Play Store ▸ your profile ▸ Payments & subscriptions ▸ Budget & order history.

We delete the record within 30 days and email you when it is done.

Deleting is not cancelling

Uninstalling Nagg does not cancel a subscription, and neither does deleting the record above — billing belongs to the store. Cancel on iPhone under Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions, and on Android under Play Store ▸ your profile ▸ Payments & subscriptions ▸ Subscriptions.
