What Nagg does

You tell Nagg something you have committed to starting, and when. At that time it rings — through Silent mode and through Focus — and it comes back up to five more times until you prove you started, by photographing your workspace, scanning a code you put on your desk, or starting a focus timer.

Nagg is a focus tool, not a safety device and not a medical one. Do not rely on it for anything where a missed alarm has serious consequences — medication, travel, or anything involving someone's safety. Phones get switched off, run out of battery, and update themselves overnight. Use a dedicated device for anything that matters that much.
