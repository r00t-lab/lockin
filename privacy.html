Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 August 2026

Nagg is an alarm that makes you prove you started. It is built so that almost nothing about you needs to leave your phone, and this page describes exactly what does.

The short version. There is no account. Your commitments never leave your device. Photos you take as proof are checked on the phone and are never uploaded or saved. The only thing sent anywhere is what is needed to run a subscription.

What stays on your device

  • Your commitments — what you committed to, when it rings, how you prove it, your streak and your missed count. Stored in Nagg's own container on the phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
  • Proof photos. When you photograph your desk, the image is examined on the device to check the frame is not empty, and then discarded. It is never uploaded, never written to your photo library, and never kept by Nagg.
  • Desk codes. A desk code is a QR image drawn on your phone from your own commitment's identifier. Nothing about it is transmitted.

What is sent, and to whom

  • Apple handles every purchase. Nagg never sees your card, your Apple Account, or your name.
  • RevenueCat manages subscriptions on our behalf. It receives an anonymous identifier generated on your device, plus the purchase receipt Apple issues, so the app can tell whether a subscription is active. It is not given your name, your email, your commitments or your photos. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Permissions Nagg asks for

  • Alarms. So a commitment can ring through Silent mode and Focus. This is the entire product; without it Nagg is a reminder you would swipe away.
  • Camera. Only to photograph your desk, or to scan your desk code, as proof that you started. Nagg cannot use the camera at any other time.

Children

Nagg is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Your data, your call

Because there is no account, there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete on your behalf. Deleting the app removes everything Nagg stored. To cancel a subscription, use Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
