Support

Usually answered within a day

Email info@kivanckarahasan.pro. Say which iPhone and which iOS version, and what you expected to happen.

The alarm did not ring

Check that Nagg has alarm permission in Settings ▸ Nagg. Then open Nagg and look at the commitment: if it says No alarm set, tap Set the alarm again. An alarm can go missing if the phone hits its limit or was restored from a backup.

It rang but the app would not open

Open Nagg yourself. If a commitment is mid-chain it takes you straight to the proof screen — you never have to use the alarm's own button.

I lost my desk code

It is on the commitment's card, behind the QR button. Print it again from there.

Restoring a subscription

Open the paywall and tap Restore. Subscriptions follow your Apple Account, so a new phone signed into the same account keeps Pro.

Cancelling

Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions on your iPhone. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
