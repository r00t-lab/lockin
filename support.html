Support

Usually answered within a day

Email info@kivanckarahasan.pro. Say which phone and which version of iOS or Android, and what you expected to happen.

Before you write: press and hold the nagg wordmark at the top of the app. That opens a diagnostics screen showing what the app actually thinks — permissions, whether the camera can start, and how many alarms are in the chain. Reading those lines out saves a round of guessing. The same screen has Test the alarm now, which is usually the faster answer.

The alarm did not ring

Make it ring on demand first — that separates "this phone will not let Nagg through" from "that one alarm did not get set", and the two have nothing to do with each other.

  • iPhone: press and hold the nagg wordmark, then tap Test the alarm now and choose a proof. Before you have added anything, the same test sits on the home screen as Try it now; it steps aside once you have a real commitment.
  • Android: Rehearse the alarm is at the bottom of the list.

Either way it rings in 20 seconds and then every 30, five times over. Put the phone down and prove nothing — that is the part worth watching. It never touches your streak.

If the test rings but a real commitment did not: open Nagg and look at that commitment. If it says No alarm set, tap Set the alarm again. An alarm can go missing if the phone hits its limit or was restored from a backup.

On iPhone, check that Nagg has alarm permission in Settings ▸ Nagg.

On Android, the usual cause is battery optimisation quietly stopping the app. The diagnostics screen has a battery exemption line — if it is not exempt, tap through and allow it. Some manufacturers keep a second, stricter list of their own under battery or app-launch settings.

It rang but the app would not open

Open Nagg yourself. If a commitment is mid-chain it takes you straight to the proof screen — you never have to use the alarm's own button.

I lost my desk code

It is on the commitment's card, behind the QR button. Print it again from there.

Restoring a subscription

Open the paywall and tap Restore. Subscriptions follow the store account that bought them — your Apple Account or your Google account — so a new phone signed into the same account keeps Pro. A subscription bought on one platform does not carry to the other, because the two stores bill separately.

Cancelling

On iPhone: Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store ▸ your profile ▸ Payments & subscriptions ▸ Subscriptions. Refunds belong to the store — Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google at your Play order history.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling removes everything Nagg kept on the phone. For the subscription record and what to send us, see deleting your data.
