Every focus app waits for you to open it.

Nagg comes to find you. Tell it what you keep putting off and when you will start. At that time it rings — through Silent, through Focus, through Do Not Disturb. Dismissing it does not work: it comes back, up to five times.

The only way to stop it is to prove you started. Photograph your desk, scan the code you taped to it, or start a 25-minute timer. Then it counts the days — and every excuse you made.

Requires iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Nagg uses AlarmKit, the same system behind Apple's own Clock app. That is why it rings when everything else stays quiet.

Nagg — an alarm you have to prove yourself to.
